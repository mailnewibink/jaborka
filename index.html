SPECIAL CARGO SUPPORT

Pre-Shipment Verification Categories

  • Beauty & Cosmetics — Skincare, makeup, and personal care formulations.
  • Food, Supplements & Powders — Packaged goods requiring ingredient and hygiene scrutiny.
  • Electronics & Batteries — Lithium cells, power banks, and electrical machinery.

Required Documents for Verification

To ensure smooth processing and avoid port hold-ups, please provide our team with:

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List (item details, gross weight, CBM).
  • Clear Product Photos showing packaging, markings, and labels.
  • MSDS / Technical Sheet for liquid, powder, or battery items.
